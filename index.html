Why Posture Matters

Small daily habits create hidden strain. Here is what happens when you restore neutral alignment.

Poor Posture (Slouching)
• Head pushed forward: Creates heavy strain on the upper spine and neck muscles.
• Rounded shoulders: Compresses the chest and restricts deep breathing.
• Tilted pelvis: Places continuous tension on the lower back and hips.
Common result: Neck stiffness, tension headaches, and afternoon fatigue.
Neutral Alignment (Balanced)
• Balanced head position: Head sits easily over the shoulders with zero neck pull.
• Open chest: Shoulders rest naturally back, allowing effortless full breaths.
• Supported lower back: Weight is distributed evenly across hips and feet.
Target outcome: Less daily aches, effortless upright poise, and more energy.
