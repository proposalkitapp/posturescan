The 4 Clinical Checkpoints We Measure

Every scan rigorously evaluates your kinetic chain against published peer-reviewed orthopedic standards.

Normal: CVA ≥50°

1. Craniovertebral Angle & Spine Strain

Measured from ear tragus to C7 vertebrae. We compute extra cervical spine compression in both pounds and kilograms (+lbs / kg) based on Dr. Kenneth Hansraj's spinal load model to prevent chronic neck pain and upper trapezius spasm.

Metric Formula: 90° - arctan(|Δy| / |Δx|)
Normal: Cobb <40°

2. Thoracic Kyphosis (Cobb Proxy)

Measures thoracic spine curvature from C7 to T12 relative to the acromion. Identifies "desk hunch", rounded shoulders, and ribcage restriction that limits overhead arm elevation and diaphragm breathing.

Metric Formula: 180° - ∠(Tragus, C7, Acromion)
Normal: Tilt ≤5°

3. Lumbopelvic Rhythm & Pelvic Tilt (APT)

Detects anterior and posterior pelvic tilt from hip-to-knee alignment. Uncovers tight hip flexors (psoas) that inhibit glute lockout, causing lower back tightness and hamstring over-recruitment.

Metric Formula: |arctan(Δx / Δy)| Hip-Knee
Normal: Valgus <3.5°

4. Dynamic Knee Tracking (Valgus Q-Angle)

Frontal plane screening of hip, knee, and ankle vectors. Identifies medial knee collapse (knock-knees) caused by weak gluteus medius, protecting against ACL tears and patellofemoral pain.

Metric Formula: |180° - ∠(Hip, Knee, Ankle)|
